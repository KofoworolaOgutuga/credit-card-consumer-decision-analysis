# Credit Card Consumer Decision Analysis

## Overview

This repository contains two academic data analytics projects focused on consumer credit card decision-making. The projects explore how marketing strategies, consumer characteristics, and predictive analytics can support business decision-making.

The projects combine data analysis, data mining, visualization, and business interpretation to examine factors that may influence credit card selection.

## Projects

### 1. The Impact of Marketing Taglines and Video Types on Credit Card Choice Decisions

This project explores how marketing taglines and video types may influence consumer preferences and credit card choice decisions.

**Key focus areas:**

* Marketing communication and consumer decision-making
* Analysis of marketing taglines and video types
* Data exploration and interpretation
* Business insights related to credit card marketing

**Project materials:**

* Written report
* PowerPoint presentation
* Python analysis in Google Colab

[View the Google Colab Notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/13N-mEzz8OvnJGvaNEm8_QYSxDNDMGurp)

### 2. Credit Card Selection Prediction for Consumer Decision-Making

This project focuses on predicting whether a respondent will select the dominant or preferred credit card using consumer characteristics, marketing variables, video types, taglines, and attention measures.

**Key focus areas:**

* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Predictive modeling and data mining
* Model interpretation and comparison
* Business insights and recommendations

**Project materials:**

* Written report
* PowerPoint presentation
* Python analysis in Google Colab
* Dataset: `cred_card.xlsx` (can not be shared)

[View the Google Colab Notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1EMYVnrWNVmNMOvoi3GhpNJFCVQm2LkHg)

## Tools and Technologies

* **Python** for data analysis and predictive modeling
* **Google Colab** for notebook-based analysis
* **Pandas and NumPy** for data manipulation, where applicable
* **Matplotlib and Seaborn** for data visualization, where applicable
* **Scikit-learn** for machine learning, where applicable
* **Microsoft Excel** for dataset handling
* **Microsoft PowerPoint** for presenting findings and recommendations

## Project Objectives

The overall objectives of these projects are to:

1. Explore factors associated with consumer credit card selection.
2. Examine the potential relationship between marketing content and consumer preferences.
3. Apply data analysis and data mining techniques to consumer decision-making.
4. Interpret analytical findings in a business context.
5. Develop practical insights that may support marketing and customer-focused decisions.
