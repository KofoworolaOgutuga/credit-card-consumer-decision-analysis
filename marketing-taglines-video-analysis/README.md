# The Impact of Marketing Taglines and Video Types on Credit Card Choice Decisions

## Overview

This academic project examines the potential impact of marketing taglines and video types on consumer credit card choice decisions.

The project investigates how different forms of marketing communication may relate to consumer preferences and decision-making. It applies data analysis to a marketing research question and presents the findings in a written report and PowerPoint presentation.

## Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

* Examine the relationship between marketing taglines and credit card choice decisions.
* Explore how different video types may relate to consumer preferences.
* Analyze the available data to identify relevant patterns.
* Interpret the findings from a marketing and business perspective.
* Communicate the analysis and findings through a written report and presentation.

## Research Focus

The project focuses on consumer decision-making in the context of credit card marketing.

The main areas of interest include:

* Marketing taglines
* Video types
* Consumer preferences
* Credit card choice decisions
* Implications for marketing communication strategies

## Methodology
